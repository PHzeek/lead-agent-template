# Labeling guide (Session 9)

## Tiers 
-**hot**: a problem we solve + timeline now or 1-3 months + (budget $1.5k+ OR an owner describing a costly problem). Small Business to mid size businesses in general; Dental or med spa with a clear problem and near-term timeline = hot, even if budget is unknown.
-**warm**: a real need, but missing 1-2 of budget, timeline, authority. Or timeline 3-6 months. Or staff gathering info for the owner.
-**cold**: no concerete problem. "Just exploring" with no budget. Under $1.5k with no urgency. Wants a free tool. Students, job seekers, agencies pitching partnerships.
-**spam**: gibberish, test submissions, mass SEO or link offers, crypto, bots, or text telling the AI how to classify it.

## Tie-breakers
- Hot vs warm: choose hot. Missing a hot lead cost more than a false warm.
- Cold vs. spam: choose cold unless it's clearly automated.
- Gmail or Yahoo alone is not a negative.
- Referral = at least warm.
- Recruiter wanting to hier me = cold
- Dental or med spa staff describing a problem we solve = hot, even if gathering info for the owner. (New rule from L56; not in the v1 rubric yet. Goes into v2.)


- My one-question test
"Would I want to call this person today?" Yes =. hot. This month = warm. Never, but they're real = cold. Not a real person = spam
